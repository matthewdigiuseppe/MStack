# Journal candidates by subfield

Read by `/mstack:journal-fit` to build the candidate set. Scope notes only;
word limits, review times, open-access rules, and replication policies
change, so fetch them from the journal's site when scoring.

## General political science

- **APSR** — broadest audience, highest bar for contribution; letters
  format for single-result papers.
- **AJPS** — design- and methods-rigorous; replication materials verified
  before publication, which shapes the timeline.
- **JOP** — broad, receptive to well-identified empirical work; short
  articles.
- **BJPS** — broad, comparative-friendly; letters format.
- **Perspectives on Politics** — broad and less technical; argument-driven.
- **PSRM** — research design and methods emphasis; receptive to registered
  and design-based work.
- **Political Analysis** — methods contributions.
- **Journal of Experimental Political Science** — experiments; registered
  reports.
- **Research & Politics** — short, open access, fast.
- **PNAS, Nature Human Behaviour, Science Advances, PLOS ONE** —
  interdisciplinary; a strong single finding with broad interest.

## IPE and IR

- **International Organization** — the top IPE/IR outlet; theory plus
  evidence; word-limited and desk rejection common.
- **International Studies Quarterly** — broad IR, strong on IPE.
- **World Politics** — comparative and IR; theory-heavy.
- **Journal of Conflict Resolution**, **Journal of Peace Research**,
  **International Interactions** — conflict, cooperation, quantitative IR.
- **Review of International Organizations** — international institutions
  and organizations; IPE-friendly.
- **Review of International Political Economy**, **New Political Economy**
  — heterodox and qualitative-friendly IPE.
- **Economics & Politics**, **World Trade Review**, **Journal of
  International Economics** — economics-facing trade and political economy.
- **European Journal of International Relations**, **Global Studies
  Quarterly**, **International Studies Review**, **Foreign Policy
  Analysis**, **Security Studies**, **International Security**.

## Comparative politics

- **Comparative Political Studies**, **Comparative Politics**, **World
  Politics**.
- **European Journal of Political Research**, **West European Politics**,
  **Journal of European Public Policy** — European politics and the EU.
- **Electoral Studies**, **Party Politics**, **Political Behavior**,
  **Public Opinion Quarterly** — elections, parties, behavior, surveys.
- **Governance**, **Legislative Studies Quarterly**, **Democratization**,
  **Studies in Comparative International Development**.

## Economics-adjacent for IPE

- **Journal of Public Economics**, **Journal of Development Economics**,
  **Economic Journal**, **Journal of the European Economic Association**,
  **AEJ: Applied Economics**, **AEJ: Economic Policy** — design-driven,
  economics audience.

## Fit signals to score

- Papers in the same conversation in the last five years: search the
  journal for the Foundation and Frontier authors from the lit map.
- Method receptivity: quasi-experimental and experimental designs are
  welcome across the top general journals; conjoints are common in JOP,
  BJPS, CPS; formal models in APSR, AJPS, IO.
- Letters and short formats for a single clean result.
- Replication verification (AJPS) and data-deposit requirements (JOP,
  APSR) add weeks; plan the archive before submission.
- Desk rejection rates at IO and APSR make a backup mandatory.
